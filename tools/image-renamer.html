Vendor Image Renamer

Upload a SKU list and a folder of vendor images. The tool matches each image to a QBP Material ID using the vendor part number or product name in the filename, then packages everything as a renamed ZIP ready to hand off.

Option A — Shared folder link (most common)

1
Open the Dropbox link the vendor sent you in your browser.
2
Click "Add to my Dropbox" (top right). This saves it to your own Dropbox account.
3
Open the Dropbox desktop app on your computer and wait for the folder to finish syncing. You'll see a checkmark when it's done.
4
Your images will be in your Dropbox folder on your computer — usually Documents → Dropbox or in your user folder.

Option B — View-only / download link

1
Open the Dropbox link in your browser.
2
Click "Download" to download the whole folder as a ZIP file.
3
Find the ZIP in your Downloads folder and double-click it to unzip. This creates a regular folder of images.
4
Use that unzipped folder in the tool below.
Then: In the Image Folder box below, click "Select Folder" and navigate to the synced or unzipped folder. The tool reads the filenames — it does not upload your images to the internet. Everything stays on your computer.
